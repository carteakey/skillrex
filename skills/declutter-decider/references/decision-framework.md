# Declutter Decision Framework & Cognitive Biases

Detailed reasoning tools for overcoming common decluttering hurdles.

---

## 1. Cognitive Biases in Clutter

### A. The Endowment Effect & Sunk Cost Fallacy
- **The Bias**: We value things more simply because we own them, and we cling to items because of what we paid in the past.
- **The Reality Check**: The market does not care what you paid retail 3 years ago. The item’s value today is only what someone is willing to pay right now, or the utility you get from using it this week. Keeping an unused item is a recurring rent on your physical space and attention.

### B. The "Fantasy Self" Bias
- **The Bias**: Keeping camping gear, obscure cooking tools, textbooks, or sports equipment because of an idealized version of who you *want* to be, rather than who you *are* today.
- **The Reality Check**: Your home should support your actual current lifestyle, not an aspirational hobby you haven’t touched in 18 months. Reclaiming your space will create more energy than hoarding aspirational gear.

### C. The "What If I Need It?" Paranoia
- **The Bias**: Fear that getting rid of a backup cord, extra bowl, or duplicate jacket will leave you stranded.
- **The Reality Check**: Apply the **20/20 Rule**: If it can be replaced in under 20 minutes for under $20, it does not warrant space in your home.

## 2. Fast Decision Matrix

| Item Category | Last Used | Resale Value | Emotional Attachment | Decision |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Electronics / Tech** | > 6 months ago | ≥ $25 | None / Low | **SELL** immediately (depreciates daily!) |
| **Electronics / Cables** | > 1 year ago | < $10 | None | **RECYCLE (E-Waste)** |
| **Clothing / Shoes** | > 1 year ago | Brand name / high | Low | **SELL** on Poshmark/Marketplace |
| **Clothing / Shoes** | > 1 year ago | Fast fashion / low | Low | **DONATE** |
| **Sentimental Items** | N/A | Variable | High | **Curate**: Keep top 10% best pieces, photograph the rest, donate/recycle |
| **Duplicates** | Mixed | Any | Low | **Keep 1 best, discard rest** |
| **Books / Media** | Read once | < $5 | Low | **DONATE** to local library/little free library |
| **Video Games** | Finished | Any | Low | **SELL** (secondary game market is active) |
