# Seller Safety & Quick-Reply Snippets

Safety protocols and canned responses to protect sellers from scams, time-wasters, and lowballers.

---

## 1. Safety & Scam Red Flags

Never engage with buyers who exhibit any of the following behaviors:
1. **The "Courier / Moving Agent" Scam**: *"I am out of town/busy, but my movers/FedEx agent will come pick it up and give you cash/check."* (100% scam).
2. **Fake Payment Emails**: Buyer asks for your email address to "send payment" and sends a fake notification saying funds are on hold until you ship or pay an upgrade fee.
3. **Six-Digit Verification Code Scam**: Buyer asks for your phone number, then asks for a 6-digit Google Voice or carrier verification code sent to your phone.
4. **Overpayment / Cashier's Checks**: Offering to pay more than asking price via check.

### Safe Transaction Rules
- **Public & Well-Lit**: Meet in your residential building lobby, inside a coffee shop, at a bank branch, or a designated police station swap zone.
- **Inspect Before Pay**: Buyer inspects the item in person; payment is transferred and verified on the spot.
- **E-Transfer Verification**: Wait until the notification shows in your actual banking app before handing over high-value electronics. Do not rely solely on a screenshot or SMS.

## 2. Quick-Reply Message Snippets

Copy-paste these into Facebook Messenger to save time:

### A. The "Is this available?" Automated Click
> *"Hi [Name], yes it's still available! When are you able to come pick it up near [Area/Cross-streets]?"*

### B. The 50% Lowball Offer
> *"Thanks for the offer, but the item is already discounted below market value for a quick sale and the price is firm. Best of luck with your search!"*

### C. The "Can You Hold It Until Next Week?" Request
> *"I can only hold items once a firm pickup time is agreed for today or tomorrow, or with a small deposit via e-Transfer. Otherwise it's first-come, first-served. Let me know when you're ready to head over!"*

### D. The "Can You Deliver?" Request
> *"I prefer local pickup near [Location]. If you are unable to pick up, I can deliver within [X km] for an extra $[15-20] gas/time fee."*

### E. The Last-Minute On-Site Haggle
> *"As mentioned in the ad, the price was set firm at $[X]. If that doesn't work for you today, no worries at all, but I won't be able to let it go for less."*
