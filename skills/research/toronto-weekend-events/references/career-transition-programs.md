# Nursing Transition — Ontario Reference

Career transition into healthcare/nursing for applicants with non-science undergraduate degrees.

## Second-Entry BScN Programs
- Focus on "Second-Entry" or "Accelerated" BScN programs (typically 2 years for those with a prior degree)
- Research "Pre-Health Science" bridge programs if missing specific sciences (Anatomy, Physiology, Microbiology)
- Key institutions: UofT Nursing, York Nursing, local colleges with accelerated streams

## Inquiry Template
- Subject: Inquiry regarding Second-Entry BScN prerequisites - [Your Name]
- Body: "Hi, I hold a Bachelor's degree in [Subject] and am interested in applying to your Second-Entry Nursing program. Could you please direct me to an academic advisor or admissions officer who can help me review my transcripts to see which specific science prerequisites I am missing? I would like to know if I can complete these via your institution or if I need to take a pre-health program first."

## Pitfalls
- Do not assume your psych degree covers all science prerequisites
- Always ask specifically about "Second-Entry" programs rather than standard 4-year programs to save time
- Keep copies of all previous course outlines/syllabi ready for credit assessment

## Relevant Support Organizations (Ontario)
- **L'Arche (International/Ontario):** Faith-based communities where people with and without disabilities live together
- **Meta Centre (GTA):** Comprehensive services for individuals with special needs
- **Camphill Communities Ontario:** Residential/farm communities focusing on shared living and work
- **Community Living Ontario:** Provincial network with local chapters (e.g., Toronto, Mississauga)
- **Developmental Services Ontario (DSO):** The primary access point for government-funded adult developmental services
- **Extend-A-Family:** Focuses on community connection and social inclusion
